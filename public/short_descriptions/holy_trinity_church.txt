The Holy Trinity Church is one of the most important religious and architectural landmarks in Svishtov. Built in the 19th century and completed in 1867, it represents the Bulgarian National Revival period under Ottoman rule. Designed by master builder Kolyu Ficheto, the church follows a three-nave basilica plan with a central dome and strong symmetrical proportions. Its exterior is marked by balanced stonework and traditional Revival-era forms, while the interior contains finely carved woodwork and iconography created by leading Bulgarian artists of the time. The church functioned not only as a place of worship but also as a symbol of cultural identity and resilience during a period of political restriction. It remains one of the strongest architectural statements of the Revival era in northern Bulgaria.
