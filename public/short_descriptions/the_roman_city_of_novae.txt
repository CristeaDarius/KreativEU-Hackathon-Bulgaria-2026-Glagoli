The Roman city of Novae lies just outside modern Svishtov and represents the earliest major urban layer of the region. Founded in the first century AD, it served as a permanent military base for Legio I Italica, one of Rome’s elite legions. The fortress controlled the Danube frontier and secured the northern border of the empire. Over time, it expanded beyond a military camp into a full city with baths, workshops, administrative buildings, and residential areas. Archaeological remains show advanced Roman engineering, including paved streets, heating systems, and water infrastructure. Novae remained important into Late Antiquity and was later reinforced during the Byzantine period before gradually declining. Today it is one of the most significant Roman archaeological sites in Bulgaria.
