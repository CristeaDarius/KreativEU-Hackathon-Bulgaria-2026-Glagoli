The D. A. Tsenov Academy of Economics is the main higher education institution in Svishtov and one of the oldest specialized economic universities in Bulgaria. It was founded in 1936 through a major donation by Dimitar Tsenov, a local merchant who left his fortune to create a school dedicated to economics and commerce. The academy was designed to train professionals in finance, accounting, trade, and administration, following European academic models of the time. Over the decades it expanded through political and economic changes, adapting from pre-war commercial education to socialist planning systems and later to modern market economics. Today it remains a major academic center, with thousands of students and strong links to Bulgaria’s economic and administrative sectors.
